# TASK

Search job vacancies matching the user's profile.

## PRIORITY

1. Official Career Page
2. LinkedIn Jobs
3. JobStreet
4. Indeed
5. Glints
6. KitaLulus
7. Kalibrr
8. Urbanhire
9. Loker.id

## FILTER

- Match skills.
- Match location.
- Match experience.
- Remove duplicates.
- Exclude expired jobs.

## COLLECT

- Company
- Position
- Location
- Employment Type
- Salary
- Apply Link
- Closing Date
- Source

## OUTPUT

| Company | Position | Location | Type | Salary | Deadline | Apply |
